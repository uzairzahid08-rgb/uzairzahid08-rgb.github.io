01 / About

About Me

I completed my MS in Geospatial Sciences at the University of Peshawar, Pakistan, with a CGPA of 3.90/4.00. My research interests include flood risk assessment, landslide and debris-flow hazards, remote sensing, spatial modelling and machine learning.

I aim to turn geospatial data into meaningful evidence that supports disaster risk reduction, environmental monitoring and informed decision-making.
